CURRENT RESEARCH

From twilight brightness to Islamic legal reasoning.

My research program connects empirical observation with normative analysis. I focus on the visibility of the young crescent under tropical twilight conditions, photometric measurement, atmospheric effects, and the implications of those findings for hisab–rukyat criteria and Hijri calendar development.

Research roadmap →
01

Twilight & Crescent Visibility

Sky brightness, contrast thresholds, atmosphere, lunar geometry, and tropical observing conditions.

02

Observational Astronomy

Digital imaging, photometry, optics, CCD/CMOS observation, and empirical crescent detection.

03

Hisab–Rukyat & Calendar

Visibility criteria, uncertainty, regional and global Hijri calendar systems, and harmonization.

04

Islamic Legal Reasoning

Fiqh, maqāṣid al-sharīʿah, scientific evidence, and contemporary Islamic legal methodology.
