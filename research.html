RESEARCH

Twilight, crescent visibility, and Islamic calendar systems.

A research program linking observational astronomy, atmospheric conditions, photometry, computational modeling, and Islamic legal reasoning.

01

Twilight (Syafaq) & Crescent Visibility

How does twilight sky brightness affect the minimum detectable contrast between the crescent Moon and the background sky? This theme examines aerosols, humidity, air mass, elongation, altitude, lunar age, and low-latitude tropical conditions.

02

Observational Astronomy & Photometry

Systematic observation using the naked eye, optics, digital imaging, CCD/CMOS systems, aperture photometry, and image-based detection to derive empirical visibility thresholds.

03

Modeling & Prediction

Numerical and probabilistic crescent-visibility models integrating geometry, atmospheric scattering, sky brightness, and observational uncertainty, including the potential role of AI-assisted detection.

04

Hisab–Rukyat & Hijri Calendar

How empirical visibility evidence can inform contemporary criteria, reduce uncertainty, and contribute to regional or global calendar harmonization.

05

Fiqh & Scientific Evidence

How scientifically modeled visibility, digital evidence, and probabilistic data interact with classical and contemporary fiqh interpretations of rukyat hilal and legal authority.
