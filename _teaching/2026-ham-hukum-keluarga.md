Graduate course in the **Islamic Family Law (Hukum Keluarga Islam)** program.

The course examines the relationship between **human rights and Islamic family law**, including marriage, divorce, women’s rights, child protection, custody (*hadhanah*), maintenance, polygamy, child marriage, and the protection of children born outside marriage.

The course also discusses the interaction between Islamic legal doctrine, Indonesian family law, constitutional court decisions, CEDAW, CRC, and contemporary human rights discourse.
