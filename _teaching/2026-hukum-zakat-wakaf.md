Undergraduate course in the **Islamic Economic Law (Hukum Ekonomi Syariah)** program.

The course covers the legal and jurisprudential foundations of **zakat and waqf**, including subjects and objects of zakat, zakat al-fitr, zakat on wealth, contemporary zakat, zakat management, principles of waqf, waqf assets, productive waqf, cash waqf, and contemporary waqf practices.

The course also examines the relationship between classical Islamic jurisprudence, Indonesian legislation, and contemporary institutional practices of zakat and waqf.
