Undergraduate course in the **Islamic Economic Law (Hukum Ekonomi Syariah)** program.

The course covers the legal and jurisprudential foundations of **zakat and waqf**, including zakat al-fitr, zakat on wealth, contemporary zakat, zakat management, principles of waqf, productive waqf, cash waqf, and contemporary waqf practices.
