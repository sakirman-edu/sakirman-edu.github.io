Master's course in the **Islamic Family Law (Hukum Keluarga Islam)** program.

The course provides an advanced introduction to **philosophy of science, scientific paradigms, and the integration of knowledge in Islamic scholarship**. It examines the ontological, epistemological, and axiological foundations of knowledge, theories of truth, modern Western and classical Islamic intellectual traditions, and contemporary models of integration-interconnection.

Particular emphasis is placed on the relevance of these philosophical perspectives to **Islamic Family Law**, including interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary approaches, contemporary issues such as ecology and ecofeminism, and the development of integrative frameworks for research and scholarly analysis.

## Course Learning Outcomes

By the end of the course, students are expected to be able to:

1. Explain and critically analyze the foundations of philosophy of science, including ontology, epistemology, axiology, theories of truth, and scientific paradigms.
2. Compare modern and contemporary Western scientific traditions with classical Islamic intellectual traditions.
3. Analyze major models of knowledge integration, including the ideas of **Ismail Raji al-Faruqi, Syed Muhammad Naquib al-Attas, and M. Amin Abdullah**.
4. Apply interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary perspectives to contemporary issues in Islamic Family Law.
5. Develop and defend an integrative conceptual framework for research in Islamic Family Law.

These learning outcomes reflect the CPMK structure stated in the RPS.


## Learning Approach

The course applies a student-centered learning approach combining:

- **Critical Reading**
- **Interactive Discussion**
- **Problem-Based Learning**
- **Project-Based Learning**
- **Case Analysis**
- **Presentations**
- **Peer Review**
- **Interdisciplinary and Transdisciplinary Analysis**

The RPS explicitly positions the course within Student-Centered Learning, discussion, problem-based learning, critical reading, presentation, project-based learning, and hybrid/blended learning.


## Key Assignments

### 1. Analysis of Truth Theories and Scientific Paradigms
Students compare theories of truth and scientific paradigms and explain their implications for the construction of knowledge.

### 2. Critical Review of Knowledge Integration Thinkers
Students conduct a comparative analysis of the ideas of **Al-Faruqi and Al-Attas** on Islamization and integration of knowledge.

### 3. Interdisciplinary Analysis of Islamic Family Law Issues
Students analyze an Islamic Family Law issue by combining legal, social, gender, cultural, or other relevant disciplinary perspectives.

### 4. Knowledge Integration Model for Islamic Family Law Research
Students design a conceptual model integrating philosophy of science with Islamic Family Law studies as a basis for a final paper or research framework.

These four main assignments follow the task design specified in the RPS.
