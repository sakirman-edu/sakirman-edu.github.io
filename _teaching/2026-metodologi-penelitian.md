Graduate course on **research methodology in Islamic economics and Islamic studies**.

The course introduces qualitative research paradigms and designs, including case study, phenomenology, ethnography, grounded theory, narrative research, document analysis, policy research, and comparative qualitative research.

Students are trained to formulate research problems, identify research gaps, develop research questions, determine units of analysis, construct novelty, and design rigorous qualitative research.
