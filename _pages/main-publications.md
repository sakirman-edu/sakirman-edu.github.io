Selected publications representing my research in **Islamic astronomy, crescent visibility, Hijri calendar studies, and contemporary Islamic law**.

## Islamic Astronomy and Hijri Calendar

### The Transformation of Rukyah Al-Hilal: Integrating Digital Imaging Technology in Islamic Moon Sighting Practices
**Malaysian Journal of Syariah and Law, 2025**

M. R. Syarif, Sakirman, A. Syatar, and N. Lela.

This study examines the transformation of *rukyah al-hilal* through the integration of digital imaging technology in contemporary Islamic lunar observation.

---

### A Semantic Literature Review on Crescent Visibility: Trends, Models, and Implications for the Islamic Calendar
**Al-Hilal: Journal of Islamic Astronomy, 2025**

Muh Rasywan Syarif, Sakirman, and Muhammad Fazlurrahman Syarif.

This article reviews major developments in crescent visibility research, including visibility models and their implications for Islamic calendar determination.

---

### The Concept of the Meeting Point of Hisab and Rukyat of the Beginning of the Hijri Month
**NIZHAM: Jurnal Studi Keislaman, 2024**

Sakirman and A. Jamil.

This study discusses the epistemological and methodological meeting point between *hisab* and *rukyat* in determining the beginning of the Hijri month.

## Islamic Law and Family Law

### Reconstructing the Philosophy of Divorce in Islamic Family Law: A Maqāṣid al-Sharī‘ah Approach to Justice
**International Conference on Islam, Law, and Society (INCOILS) Conference Proceedings, 2025**

Sakirman, Evy Septiana Rachman, and Wasthanul Karim.

This paper reconstructs the philosophy of divorce in Islamic family law through the framework of *maqāṣid al-sharī‘ah*, with particular attention to justice and family protection.

---

### Reforming Islamic Family Law: The Relevance of Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyyah’s Concept of Legal Change
**Jurnal Ilmiah Mizani: Wacana Hukum, Ekonomi dan Keagamaan, 2024**

Ayu Sari Ningsih, Nawa Angkasa, Nency Dela Oktora, Sakirman, Nyimas Lidya Putri Pertiwi, and Ananto Triwibowo.

This article examines the relevance of Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyyah’s theory of legal change for contemporary reform of Islamic family law.
