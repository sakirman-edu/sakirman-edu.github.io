Selected publications representing my research in **Islamic astronomy, crescent visibility, Hijri calendar studies, and contemporary Islamic law**.

## Islamic Astronomy and Hijri Calendar

### [The Transformation of Rukyah Al-Hilal: Integrating Digital Imaging Technology in Islamic Moon Sighting Practices](/publication/2025-transformation-rukyah-al-hilal)
**Malaysian Journal of Syariah and Law, 13(1), 314–324, 2025**

Muhammad Rasywan Syarif, Sakirman, Abdul Syatar, and Nur Lela.

This study examines the transformation of *rukyah al-hilal* through the integration of digital imaging technology in contemporary Islamic lunar observation.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.33102/mjsl.vol13no1.751)

---

### [A Semantic Literature Review on Crescent Visibility: Trends, Models, and Implications for the Islamic Calendar](/publication/2025-semantic-literature-review-crescent-visibility)
**Al-Hilal: Journal of Islamic Astronomy, 7(1), 67–88, 2025**

Muh Rasywan Syarif, Sakirman Sakirman, and Muhammad Fazlurrahman Syarif.

This article reviews major developments in crescent visibility research, including visibility models and their implications for Islamic calendar determination.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.21580/al-hilal.2025.7.1.26099)

---

### [The Concept of the Meeting Point of Hisab and Rukyat of the Beginning of the Hijri Month](/publication/2024-meeting-point-hisab-rukyat)
**Nizham: Jurnal Studi Keislaman, 12(1), 122–138, 2024**

Sakirman Sakirman and A Jamil.

This study discusses the conceptual and methodological meeting point between *hisab* and *rukyat* in determining the beginning of the Hijri month.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.32332/nizham.v12i01.9117)

---

### [Integrasi Hisab Rukyat Awal Ramadan 1442 H dengan Model Visibilitas Kastner](/publication/2022-integrasi-hisab-rukyat-kastner)
**ELFALAKY: Jurnal Ilmu Falak, 6(2), 278–297, 2022**

Sakirman Sakirman, Judhistira Aria Utama, and Othman bin Zainon.

This study integrates *hisab* and *rukyat* through Kastner's crescent visibility model to evaluate the astronomical feasibility of crescent observations.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.24252/ifk.v6i2.30766)

---

### [Respon Fikih terhadap Perkembangan Teknologi Rukyat](/publication/2020-respon-fikih-teknologi-rukyat)
**Al-Manahij: Jurnal Kajian Hukum Islam, 14(1), 69–86, 2020**

Sakirman.

This article examines Islamic legal responses to telescopes, digital imaging, and other technological developments in contemporary crescent observation.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.24090/mnh.v14i1.3190)

## Islamic Law and Family Law

### [Reforming Islamic Family Law: The Relevance of Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyyah's Concept of Legal Change](/publication/2025-reforming-islamic-family-law)
**Jurnal Ilmiah Mizani: Wacana Hukum, Ekonomi dan Keagamaan, 12(2), 392–405, 2025**

Ayu Sari Ningsih, Nawa Angkasa, Nency Dela Oktora, Sakirman Sakirman, Nyimas Lidya Putri Pertiwi, and Ananto Triwibowo.

This article examines the relevance of Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyyah's theory of legal change for contemporary reform of Islamic family law.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.29300/mzn.v12i1.7087)

---

### [Reconstructing the Philosophy of Divorce in Islamic Family Law: A Maqāṣid al-Sharī‘ah Approach to Justice](/publication/2025-reconstructing-philosophy-divorce)
**Conference Paper, 5th INCOILS 2025, Yogyakarta**

Sakirman, Evy Septiana Rachman, and Wastahnul Karim.

This paper reconstructs the philosophy of divorce in Islamic family law through the framework of *maqāṣid al-sharī‘ah*, with particular attention to substantive justice and family protection.
