<h2>Research Focus</h2>

<p>
  My current research focuses on the relationship between
  <strong>twilight conditions, sky brightness, atmospheric conditions,
  and crescent visibility in tropical and low-latitude environments</strong>.
  It integrates observational astronomy, digital imaging, photometric analysis,
  astronomical modeling, and Islamic legal reasoning to contribute to the
  development of <strong>evidence-based crescent visibility criteria and
  scientifically informed Hijri calendar systems</strong>.
</p>
