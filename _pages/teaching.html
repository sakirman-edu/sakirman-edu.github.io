Academic Teaching

My teaching focuses on Islamic law, Islamic astronomy, research methodology, academic writing, and interdisciplinary approaches to contemporary Islamic studies at undergraduate, master's, and doctoral levels.

Courses integrate theoretical foundations, critical analysis, practical training, research-based learning, and engagement with contemporary legal, social, astronomical, and methodological issues.
