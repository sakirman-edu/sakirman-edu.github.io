This article examines the **genealogy of Islamic astronomy (ilmu falak)** and its development within the study of Islamic law.

The study discusses the historical relationship between astronomy, science, and Islamic jurisprudence, and explains how astronomical knowledge developed from classical Islamic scientific traditions into contemporary applications related to *hisab*, *rukyat*, and Islamic religious practices.
