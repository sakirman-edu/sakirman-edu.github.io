This study examines **KH. Ahmad Dahlan's movement to correct qibla direction in Indonesia**, particularly his methodological approach in addressing the orientation of the Kauman Mosque in Yogyakarta.

The article highlights the use of **modern astronomical reasoning** in Islamic religious reform and shows how scientific calculation was employed to reassess established religious practices related to qibla direction.
