This paper reconstructs the philosophy of divorce in Islamic family law through the framework of **maqāṣid al-sharī‘ah**.

The study emphasizes substantive justice in contemporary divorce cases and examines how the objectives of Islamic law can provide a more responsive philosophical foundation for the protection of rights and family welfare.
