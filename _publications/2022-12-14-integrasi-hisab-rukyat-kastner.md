This study examines the determination of the beginning of **Ramadan 1442 H** by integrating *hisab* and *rukyat* through **Kastner's crescent visibility model**.

The analysis evaluates the astronomical feasibility of reported crescent observations and demonstrates how quantitative visibility modeling can contribute to the assessment of *rukyatul hilal* evidence and the refinement of Hijri calendar criteria.
