This study examines the transformation of **rukyah al-hilal** through the integration of digital imaging technology in contemporary Islamic moon-sighting practices.

The article discusses the role of astronomical observation and digital imaging in improving the documentation and analysis of the young crescent Moon, while considering its relevance to contemporary Islamic calendar determination.
