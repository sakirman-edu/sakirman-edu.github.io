This study examines research trends, prediction models, and the scientific and social implications of **hilal visibility** in Islamic calendar determination.

The study highlights the development of approaches ranging from classical astronomical criteria to **data-driven modeling, machine learning, and digital imaging technology**, and emphasizes the importance of collaboration between astronomers and Islamic scholars in developing accurate and broadly acceptable Hijri calendar systems.
