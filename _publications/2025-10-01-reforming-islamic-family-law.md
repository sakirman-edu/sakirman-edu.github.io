This study examines **Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyyah's concept of legal change** and its relevance to contemporary reform of Islamic family law.

The article argues that Islamic family law can respond to changing social contexts while remaining grounded in Islamic legal principles. Ibn Qayyim's framework provides a theoretical basis for contextualizing legal rulings according to changes in time, place, circumstances, motives, and custom.
